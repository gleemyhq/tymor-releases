# Tymor 1.0.6

## ✨ What's new

- Refreshed light color palettes with richer Jewel Tones, a separate Deep & Muted collection, and teal-led Aurora. Uniform Warm White, Pearl White, Amber and Ember options join more intentional seasonal color combinations and clearer palette names.
- Added Christmas, Halloween, Winter and Romance shortcuts beside “Your mix,” just before the shape count. Each selects a ready-made bulb combination you can customize further, keeping your colors and other lighting settings unchanged. Your chosen bulbs are saved independently of the shortcuts, so future preset updates won't alter your mix.
- Choose a color for each bulb shape directly from its color dot in “Your mix”—for example, green trees and white stars. Choices are remembered separately for each palette and apply to every bulb of that shape. The picker shows each distinct color once, and color controls stay hidden for single-color palettes.
- Added menu bar toggles for screen and window lights, shortcuts to light settings and wallpapers, and a Performance submenu for frame rate and power-saving options.

## 🐞 Bug fixes

- The menu bar now explains when power saving pauses lights, including when weather effects are turned off.
