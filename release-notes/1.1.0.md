# Tymor 1.1.0

## ✨ What's new

- Send Santa from the menu bar or enable automatic visits in Winter settings. Choose Sky Delivery, Snow Trail, or Dock Visit, or let Santa surprise you. Santa leaves presents in different spots along the way. Click a present to open it, Option-click to remove it, or clear all presents from the menu bar.
- Bring the yeti in from the menu bar or enable automatic visits in Winter settings. The yeti can leap onto the Dock and throw snowballs. Choose whether it aims at snowmen, presents, or your screen.

## ⚡ Improvements

- The first snow on a ledge gathers in soft clumps and gradually fills into a drift. Snow and rain follow rounded edges more closely on windows, the Dock, and desktop items.
- Snow, rain, fireflies, and celebration lights use less graphics memory. Muted or inactive soundscapes release their audio from memory.
- Weather and lights release graphics resources while your Mac is locked or the display is asleep, then pick up where they left off.

## 🐞 Bug fixes

- Rain on windows fades back in after a move or resize, so streams do not linger at the old edges.
- Reduced flicker while dragging snowballs and snowmen or resizing snow-covered windows. Deep drifts shed snow in fewer particles during a resize.
