# Tymor 1.1.5

## ✨ What's new

- Decorate your desktop with Christmas trees. Add them from the menu bar or Winter settings, then drag them anywhere you like.
- Yeti can chop down trees rooted in the bottom snow bed or snowy Dock top. Control tree cutting in Yeti settings; visits still follow your automatic or manual preferences.
- Yeti now works through all reachable trees and eligible snowmen before celebrating, with varied surprise, jump, and smile endings.
- The snowplow knocks down bottom-area trees and carries them offscreen alongside presents.
