# Tymor 1.1.6

## ✨ What's new

- Five winter wallpapers now follow the time of day: Christmas Cottage, The Winter Post, Dress Code: Red, Snowy Red Tram, and The Last Skate. In Settings or during setup, choose Dynamic to follow local time, or Still to keep your favorite morning, day, evening, or night scene.
- A smaller setup window lets you preview Winter, Rain, or Fireflies directly on your desktop before activating Tymor. The preview pauses when you minimize, hide, or close setup and resumes when you bring it back. Wallpaper choices take effect after activation.

## ⚡ Improvements

- If you previously chose Cozy Cabin, Snowy Hut, Christmas Village, or Riverside Cabin, Tymor now selects Dynamic Christmas Cottage on upgrade. If you chose Snowy Red Tram, you keep its original evening scene.
