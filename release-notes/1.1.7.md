# Tymor 1.1.7

## ✨ What's new

- Hold Option and roll a snowball into another to push it along. Keep rolling to push a whole row.

## ⚡ Improvements

- Santa’s reindeer now gallop slightly out of step with each other, and their hooves settle into the snow on the Dock and along the bottom of the screen.
- Snow rides along on gentle upward window drags and hops higher with a brisk flick. Lowering a window releases a wider cloud of powder.
- The Dock and screen-bottom beds now collect 40% of the snow that lands from sweeping, moving, resizing, closing, or minimizing a window, or from a broken snowball. Snowball impacts build a mound and spread excess snow around its base.
- The snowplow crosses the screen a little faster.

## 🐞 Bug fixes

- Reduced flicker during Santa’s flight, when dragging snowballs, snowmen, or trees, and when snowballs roll or fall quickly.
- After a short downward drag, the window can catch powder that previously slipped through its top edge.
- The Yeti now aims glass throws from its throwing spot, so the snowball travels in the direction of the throw. Short throws follow a shallower arc.
- Closed small visible gaps between rolling snowballs and where a snowman rests on its snow bed.
