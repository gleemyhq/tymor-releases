# Tymor 1.0

First release.

## ✨ What's new

- Bring your desktop to life with winter snow, rain, or glowing fireflies.
- Snow gathers on windows, the Dock, desktop icons, widgets, and the bottom of your screen. Build snowmen or clear the ground with a snowplow.
- Make a rainy day your own with optional thunder, lightning, and sound.
- Move your pointer near fireflies to scatter them, or hold Option to draw them closer.
- Choose wallpapers for each ambience, with full-size images downloaded when needed.
- Adjust the frame rate and pause effects on battery or in Low Power Mode. Open Tymor automatically at login.
- Enjoy a three-day free trial; one license covers up to three Macs.
- Receive updates through Tymor’s built-in updater.
