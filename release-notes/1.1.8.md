# Tymor 1.1.8

## ✨ What's new

- Dress your snowman in a matching crown and ribbon. Choose from five top hat palettes, starting with Midnight & Slate, or use the color wheel to pick both colors yourself.
- Choose a solid Cardinal scarf or one of four striped designs, including Peppermint and Cocoa & Clementine. You can also pick your own main yarn and stripe colors.
- Mix hat and scarf colors independently. Click either shuffle button again to try another pairing in the preview. Each snowman on the Dock keeps its own mix.
- Choose your snowplow in Winter settings. The new Vintage Alpine Groomer joins the original Amber Ridge Groomer, with artwork for both in the style picker.

## ⚡ Improvements

- A larger snowman preview makes ribbons and scarf stripes easier to see. Its carrot, branches, and scarf now use the same shapes as the snowman you build on the Dock.
- The top hat has a gently tilted crown and a rounded brim that sits more naturally on the snowman’s head.
- Winter settings now group Santa, the Yeti, and Christmas trees under **Visitors & Gifts**. Settings search opens the relevant winter tab.

## 🐞 Bug fixes

- Fixed a sliver of snow showing above the top hat’s brim and a blue oval appearing between the snowballs in the settings preview.
