# Tymor 1.0.7

## ✨ What's new

- Hold Option and sweep through snow to roll snowballs on windows and the Dock, as well as along the bottom of your screen.
- Preview Christmas screen lights with Snow during setup, with a simple switch to turn them on or off.

## ⚡ Improvements

- Lower memory use for lights and weather effects, including when ambience is paused or turned off.
- Gentler cursor brushing removes less snow and stays consistent across frame rates.
- Falling snow keeps its fullness when windows close or minimize.
- Snowballs and snowmen leave fuller piles of snow where they break apart.
- Redesigned setup with an immersive ambience preview, a wallpaper gallery, and a more cohesive Menu Bar screen.

## 🐞 Bug fixes

- Fixed flickering or stretched screen lights when quickly toggling edges.
- Fixed a crash that could occur while lights or weather effects were active.
- Snow now breaks away at the right moment when minimizing a window, without following it into the Dock or falling early.
- Fixed detached snow beds during trackpad swipes between Spaces, including slow and canceled swipes.
- Fixed gaps in partially covered snow beds and improved how snow meets rounded window corners.
