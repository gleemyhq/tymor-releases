# Tymor 1.2.0

## ✨ What's new

- Choose between the classic decorated Christmas tree and the new Snowy Spruce in Winter settings.
- Penguin chicks visit in flocks, leaving footprints as they waddle through the snow. They belly-slide past one another and hop on and off the Dock. Click a walking chick and it waves hello.
- When the snowplow approaches, chicks turn and slide out of its path or take shelter on the Dock. If the plow blocks their way down, they line up on the edge until it passes.
- Choose how many penguins visit in Winter settings. Turn on **Automatic penguin visits** or select **Bring Penguins Now** to call the flock.

## ⚡ Improvements

- Snow along the bottom of the screen has gentler ripples for a smoother belly slide over the drifts.
