# Tymor 1.1.2

## ✨ What's new

- Aim falling snow and rain with the **Wind** dial in General settings. Drag the needle or use the arrow keys to adjust the angle. Choose **Use automatic** to bring back the natural drift.
- Weather pauses on each display covered by a fullscreen app to save CPU and memory. When you return to the desktop, falling particles resume where they left off and accumulated snow stays in place.

## ⚡ Improvements

- Snow, rain, and fireflies use less CPU. Santa and Yeti visits also take less work to render.
- Moving windows and switching seasons are smoother, including when a window sheds snow or you drag it through falling rain.

## 🐞 Bug fixes

- Fixed a flash that could appear over nearby windows when you moved or resized a window in the rain.
- Fixed rain streams that lingered after a window moved or closed. Streams now taper smoothly at window edges.
- Fixed flickering in the snow that falls when you close a window.
