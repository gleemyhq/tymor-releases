# Tymor 1.1.3

## ✨ What's new

- Give a snow-covered window a quick upward drag to send a flurry over its top edge. The flakes drift back down when the window stops.

## 🐞 Bug fixes

- Weather stays visible when you take a screenshot, start a screen recording, or drag a desktop icon.
- Snow follows desktop icons more closely as you move them, and the snow on the Dock adjusts sooner when you remove an icon.
- Weather stays out of the way when Finder or another app enters full screen.
- Snow on nearby windows stays visible and steady while you drag a window.
