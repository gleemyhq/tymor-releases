# Tymor 1.0.2

## ✨ What's new

- Dress finished snowmen with a cap: choose Off, Top Hat, Santa, or Mix (the default — a random cap per snowman) in Winter settings, with the settings preview showing the chosen cap.
- Snowmen feel more alive: their coal eyes blink now and then, the scarf knots at the front and its hanging end sways gently into a short fringe, soft shadows settle where the snowballs rest on each other, and branch arms gained an extra twig.
- The snow plow is redrawn as a tracked groomer with a detailed cab and blade, and it now throws a spray of powder and clumps off the blade as it pushes snow.
- Rain falls more naturally: drops accelerate to their own speed, streaks stretch as they gather pace, and the shared wind eases, gusts, and drifts the rain into moving bands.
- Rain now splashes where drops actually land — on window ledges, the Dock, and the ground — and window-edge streams gather into beads that swell, let go, and run down the side.

## 🐞 Bug fixes

- Snow on a window's top now breaks up and falls promptly and in one piece when the window is closed or minimized, instead of lingering or clinging to the shrinking window.
- Powder swept off a moved window no longer tunnels through or lands back on its own top, and snow beds on sideways-slid windows now cover the newly exposed stretch so it starts bare and catches snow properly.
